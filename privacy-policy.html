NeuroSOS — Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last Updated: October 2026

1. 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection Policy (Google Play Families & COPPA Compliant)

NeuroSOS operates 100% offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, share, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, microphone audio, or camera data.

  • Zero External Servers: All user preferences and optional session notes are stored exclusively on your local device using browser LocalStorage / SQLite and never leave your device unless you manually export a CSV file.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers or Ads: NeuroSOS contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no third-party network requests.

2. Wellness & Non-Medical Disclaimer

NeuroSOS is a general wellness, sensory comfort, relaxation, and focus timer application. It is not a medical device, nor is it intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any disease, medical condition, or neurodevelopmental disorder.

The audio and visual features provided in NeuroSOS are for general calming, ambient sound masking, and task-transition support only. Nothing in this application constitutes medical, psychological, occupational therapy, or behavioral health advice. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional with any questions regarding a medical or behavioral condition.

3. Intellectual Property Declaration

All audio waveforms (Brownian noise, Pink noise, pentatonic chimes, and rhythmic pulses) and visual animations in NeuroSOS are synthesized mathematically in real time using standard open-source digital signal processing (DSP) equations and procedural WebGL shaders. NeuroSOS is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any third-party therapy protocol or certification board.
